Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Beenetic Studios makes games for mobile devices. This policy explains what information those games and this website collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to every Beenetic Studios app and site. Where a particular app does something specific, that is called out by name.

The short version: we collect the little we need to run online play, keep your progress, tell you when a game is waiting, fix crashes, and show ads. We do not sell your information, and you can delete your account and its data at any time.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • A display name, an avatar, an optional one-line status, and a country, which you choose in the app. The country starts out pre-selected from your device's settings, and you can change it. Other players you are matched with can see these.
  • A report, if you report another player's name or status. See Names, statuses and reports below.
  • Anything you write to us, if you send us an email.

Information created by playing

  • Your account identifier. When you first play, the app creates an anonymous identifier for your device. If your device is signed in to Google Play Games, the app links your Play Games player identifier to that account automatically, so your progress survives a new device. You can also sign in to Play Games from inside the app to link it.
  • Your online match record (wins, losses, draws) and the state of games you are currently playing online: the moves made, whose turn it is, and when each move happened.
  • Your progress, kept with your account so it follows you to a new device: your coin balance and a record of recent coin earnings and purchases (including when you claimed a reward by watching an ad), the items you own, puzzle and lesson progress, your daily puzzle streak, and which computer opponents you have beaten.
  • A history of your finished games, including games against the computer: the result, the moves of recent games, and, for online games, your opponent's name, avatar and account identifier. Also your win, loss and draw totals against the computer.
  • Your invite code, which other players can enter to start a game with you.
  • The players you have blocked: each one's account identifier, the display name they had when you blocked them, and when. Only you see this list, and a player is never told you blocked them.
  • For online games, a count of how many games you have finished against each opponent each day, which we use to detect abuse of rewards.
  • Your in-app preferences, such as the theme, board and piece styles you have selected, and the date your account was created.

A few things stay on your device and are never sent to us: sound, animation and board display settings, and saved game analysis. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Information your device sends automatically

  • Your device's language and time zone, a country code (from your SIM card or mobile network where available, otherwise your device's region setting), and the app's version, which are sent when you sign in. We use the time zone to work out your local day for daily rewards and streaks, and to keep notifications out of night-time hours.
  • If notifications are on, a notification token for your device. See Notifications below.
  • On Android, unless you turn them off, crash and error reports. See Crash reports below.

Information collected automatically for advertising

Our games are free and are supported by ads. To serve them, Google receives your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, basic information about your device, and information about how you interacted with an ad. See Advertising below.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your email address, phone number, date of birth, or real name.
  • We do not use a precise location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone, or your camera.
  • We do not use a third-party analytics SDK in our games. Our only advertising SDK is Google AdMob, which can use your device's advertising identifier as described in Advertising below; no other SDK in our games tracks you across other companies' apps or websites. We do use Google's Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, described below. The Firebase libraries we use for notifications and crash reports bring Google's analytics component with them; we switch its data collection off and never call it. Google Play and Apple give us aggregated crash and performance reports for our own apps as part of distributing them, which we use to fix bugs.
  • Our games contain no purchases, so we never see payment information.

How we use it

  • To run online play: pairing you with an opponent, carrying moves between you, and settling the result.
  • To keep your account and your progress attached to you across sessions and devices, and to award coins and items fairly.
  • To tell you, with one short notification, when an online game is waiting for you.
  • To show your name, avatar, status, country, and record to opponents in a match.
  • To show ads, and to record when you claim an ad reward so that it is paid once.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To keep offensive names and statuses out of the game, and to review reports about them.
  • To answer your support messages and to investigate cheating, abuse, or technical faults.

We do not build advertising or marketing profiles ourselves, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob as our only advertising provider. Ads are optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Google may use your device's advertising identifier and related information to select ads, to measure them, and to limit how often you see the same one. Google's handling of that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app shows a consent form before any personalized advertising data is used, and remembers your answer. Wherever that form applies, you can change your answer at any time from Ad Privacy Options in the app's settings. On iOS, the system also asks separately whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites, and declining that is respected regardless of anything else.

You can limit ad personalization outside our apps too. On Android, use Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising identifier and manage other ad privacy settings. On iOS, use Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

Declining personalized ads does not remove ads and does not restrict anything in the game. You will see less relevant ones, and Google still receives the information described above to deliver and measure them.

Notifications

Reversi Rivals can notify you when an online game needs you: an opponent has moved, someone has invited you to a game, a game has ended, or a game has waited 27 days for your move and will soon end because of it. We keep these few on purpose: news is gathered into at most one summary while you are away from the app, each game's time-out warning is sent once, no more than three notifications go out in a day, and none between 22:00 and 08:00 your local time, as worked out from the time zone your device last reported.

To address a notification to your device, the app gets a notification token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and stores it with your account on our backend. Our backend also keeps a short record of what is waiting for you (up to ten recently finished games, each with the opponent's name and account identifier), which games you have already seen or been warned about, when you were last in the app, and when it last notified you. The list of finished games is cleared once you have been notified about them or have seen them. The notification itself carries a short message, such as an opponent's display name and that it is your move, plus identifiers the app uses to open the right game, and is delivered by Google.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the app, under Settings, then Online, then Push Notifications, or in your device's notification settings. Turning them off in the app deletes the notification token, so nothing more can be sent to that device, and removes it from your account.

Crash reports

On Android, when Reversi Rivals crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics: what went wrong and where in the code, which screen was open, recent app log lines, your device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. We remove account identifiers, invite codes and names from those log lines before they leave your device. Reports are not linked to your account, and Firebase deletes them after 90 days. Crash reports are on unless you turn them off, under Settings, then Privacy, then Send Crash Reports.

If you choose Report a Problem in Settings, the app prepares a file and your device shares it with us through the app you pick, usually email. The file holds your device model, operating system, app version and account identifier, and the app's recent log, which can include your display name and the names and identifiers of players you have played. We use it only to look into the problem you reported.

Names, statuses and reports

Because other players can see your display name and status, the game checks both against a list of offensive words, in the app and again on our backend, before they are saved. A name or status that fails the check is not saved, and the check is done by us, without sending your text to anyone else.

If you report another player's name or status as offensive, we keep a record of the report: your account identifier, their account identifier, the name and status their profile showed at the time, and when you reported it. We use it only to review the report and, where needed, to change that player's name or remove their status. If we do, we keep the removed text with the record so it cannot simply be set again. We never tell a player who reported them. Every report is deleted automatically after 90 days, and straight away if either account is deleted.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Online matches: while the match is active, and afterwards as a completed record for both players. If you delete your account, a short record of each online game you started that ended recently is kept for 30 days so your opponent's game can record the result (see "Deleting your account and your data").
  • Notification records: the token for as long as notifications are on; the list of finished games only until you have been notified about them or have seen them.
  • Player reports: 90 days, or until the account of either player is deleted, whichever comes first.
  • Crash reports: 90 days, after which Firebase deletes them.
  • Support email, including Report a Problem files: for as long as it takes to resolve your question, and then for a reasonable period afterwards in case you write again.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own retention rules, not ours.

An account that is never linked to Google Play Games exists only for the device that created it. Uninstalling the app removes that device's copy of the identifier, which means the account can no longer be reached, but it does not by itself delete the account. Use one of the routes below if you want it gone.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account from inside the app: open Settings and, under Account, tap Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm. You need to be online. This deletes the account and its stored data from our backend and clears the game's data on that device, apart from its diagnostic log files, which are replaced as you keep playing and removed when you uninstall. It is not a sign-out and it cannot be undone. Online games you are still playing end as resignations, and invitations you sent or received are withdrawn.

If you cannot reach the in-app option, for example because you no longer have the device, you can ask us instead. See Delete your account for the request route, or write to support@beeneticstudios.com. We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

What deletion cannot reach: games you already played against another player remain in that player's own records, because they are theirs too. That includes their match history, which keeps the display name and avatar you had when the game was played, and, for games they started, the game itself until it expires on our backend. Their own short-lived records can also hold your display name and account identifier for a while: the list of finished games waiting in their notifications (cleared once they are notified or catch up), and that day's count of games played against each opponent. If another player blocked you, their block list keeps your account identifier and the display name you had then until they unblock you or delete their own account.

So that your opponents' games still record their result, deleting your account keeps a short record of each online game you started that ended in the last 30 days, including any your deletion ended. It holds the game's moves and result and the profile details a match shows (display name, avatar, country and status), is used only so your opponent's game can record the result, can be read only by that game's players, and is deleted automatically 30 days after your account is.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name, avatar, status, and country at any time in the app, turn notifications and crash reports off, decline or withdraw advertising consent where it is offered, and delete your account as described above.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to receive a copy of it, and to object to or restrict certain uses. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, our legal bases are performance of a contract (running the game and your account), consent (personalized advertising), and our legitimate interests (keeping the game working and free of abuse). In California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by state law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

To exercise any of these, write to support@beeneticstudios.com. Because we do not hold an email address or a real name for you, we may need something from inside the app, such as your display name and account identifier, to be confident we are acting on the right account. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our games are not directed to children. They are intended for players aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent age of consent where you live. If you believe a child has provided us with information, write to support@beeneticstudios.com and we will delete the account.

Security

Traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit, and account data is held by our backend provider under its own security controls. No system is perfect, but we keep the amount of information we hold small on purpose, which is the most reliable protection we can offer you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. This address will not change.
