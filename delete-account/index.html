Delete Your Account

Applies to all Beenetic Studios games, including Reversi Rivals

You can delete your account and the data attached to it at any time. There are two routes: from inside the game, which is immediate, or by email, for when you no longer have the device.

From inside the game

  1. Open the game and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete Account to confirm.

You need to be online. This is immediate and cannot be undone. It is not a sign-out: the account itself is removed, and the game's data on that device is cleared with it (apart from its diagnostic log files, which are replaced as you keep playing). Online games you are still playing end as resignations, and invitations you sent or received are withdrawn. If you keep playing afterwards, the game starts a fresh account.

By email

Write to support@beeneticstudios.com with the subject Account deletion request, and tell us:

  • Which game the account is for.
  • Your display name in that game, and your country if you set one.
  • Your account identifier, if you can still open the game: it is the Player ID under Settings, then About Reversi Rivals. It is the surest way to match the right account, but it is not required.

We do not hold an email address for you, so we may write back asking for something else that identifies the account. That is to make sure we are deleting the right one, not to delay you. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and the identifier behind it, including the link to Google Play Games if you made one.
  • Your display name, avatar, status, country, and invite code.
  • Your progress, coins and owned items, your game history and statistics, and your notification token and notification record.
  • Your online record, and the online games you started.
  • The game's data on the device you delete from, apart from its diagnostic log files.

What stays

  • Games you already played against another player remain in that player's own records, because they are theirs as well: their match history keeps the display name and avatar you had when the game was played, and games they started stay until they expire on our backend.
  • For a short while, your display name and account identifier in your opponents' own notification and daily game-count records, until those clear.
  • For 30 days, a short record of each online game you started that ended recently, so your opponent's game can still record the result. Only that game's players can read it. It holds the moves, the result, and the profile details a match shows (display name, avatar, country and status), and is then deleted automatically.
  • On the device, the game's diagnostic log files, until they are replaced as you keep playing or you uninstall the game.
  • Crash reports, which are not linked to your account and which Firebase deletes after 90 days.
  • Emails you sent us, including any Report a Problem files, until we have finished dealing with them.
  • Data held by Google for advertising, which is governed by Google's own policies rather than ours. See the Advertising section of our privacy policy for how to reset your advertising identifier.
